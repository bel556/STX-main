About STX Auto-école

STX Auto-école makes it easier for future drivers in Algeria
to find the right driving school. Our goal is to make this process faster, clearer, and more efficient

Our Mission

STX Driving School simplifies the search for driving schools for future drivers. Our goal is to make this process faster, clearer, and more efficient

We provide all the tools you need to compare, choose, and contact the ideal driving school.

Our Values

Transparency

Clear, reliable, and verified information to help you make the right choice.

Accessibility

A simple and intuitive platform, accessible on all devices.

Quality

We select only driving schools that meet our high standards.

Innovation

We innovate to constantly improve your user experience.

Our Story

STX Driving School was born from a frustration: it was difficult to find reliable information about driving schools in Algeria.

In 2025, our team decided to create a modern, complete, and intuitive solution.

What Makes Us Different

Advanced Search

Filters by city, license type, prices, and much more.

Verified Driving Schools

All schools are carefully reviewed and verified.

Authentic Reviews

Real reviews from former students to help you make an informed decision.

Price Comparison
